# Beating PewBeam — Reveal Differentiators

A living list of where Reveal wins and where we can press the advantage.
Last updated: 2026-08-09.

> Positioning note (PRD Aug 2026 correction): **do not** lead with design/theming
> as the wedge. The real differentiators are zero-install PWA, congregant
> follow-along, and liturgical/Catechism mode. Better website + better content

---

## 1. Primary wedges (lead with these)

1. **Zero-install PWA.** Opens in any browser, installs to the home screen, works
   offline. No app store, no per-seat license, no "log in to start the service."
   A church can be live in under a minute on any device already in the room.
2. **Congregant follow-along.** The verse the preacher is on mirrors in real time
   to a second device (phone/tablet on the same network) — the congregation
   follows along on their own screens. PewBeam-style tools stop at "projector
   only"; Reveal closes the gap between stage and pew.
3. **Liturgical / Catechism mode.** Built for Anglican / Catholic worship — not
   just evangelical song lyrics. Responsive to the UK church calendar and
   catechetical structure, not retro-fitted from a US worship-band workflow.

## 2. Where Reveal is simply better

4. **Better website.** Cleaner, faster, more trustworthy marketing site. The site
   itself signals craft — it doesn't look like church software from 2014.
5. **Better content.** Real, useful workflows and guides (Mikey's AI Design HQ
   heritage) instead of a bare feature grid. We teach churches *how* to run a
   better service, not just *what buttons to press*.
6. **UK market first.** Built for UK churches from day one — Salford Young Adults
   is the proving ground, Anglican context is the default. PewBeam reads as
   US-centric; we own the UK narrative.
7. **Live preacher → verse detection.** Whisper dictation auto-detects the verse
   being preached — no operator clicking through a Bible mid-sermon.
8. **Honest, calm operator console.** Designed for the person *running* the
   service: quiet, technical, status-at-a-glance. Not a generic slide editor
   wearing a "church" skin.
9. **Real metrics.** Display latency is measured end-to-end (confirm → projector
   commits), not faked from model-load time. Operators see the truth.
10. **AI-native lineage.** Mikey's AI Design HQ DNA means the product compounds —
    workflows, detection, and content all improve on a continuous loop.

## 3. Ways to push further on PewBeam (opportunities)

- **Congregant mode as a product surface, not an afterthought.** Give it its own
  branded URL/QR flow: "scan to follow along" projected on the wall.
- **Liturgy packs.** Ship ready-made Anglican/Catholic service orders as
  one-tap templates — PewBeam makes you build from scratch.
- **UK pricing & VAT clarity.** Localise the commercial story (£, Gift Aid
  language for churches, CofE diocese-friendly onboarding).
- **Content engine.** A regular UK-church-facing newsletter/guide series
  (AI Design HQ style) that PewBeam's static blog can't match.
- **Catechism / lectionary integration.** Tie verses to the liturgical calendar
  so the right reading surfaces automatically on the right Sunday.

## 4. What we must NOT do

- Don't lead with "our app looks prettier." Design is table stakes, not the
  wedge (PRD correction).
- Don't claim PewBeam weaknesses we haven't verified — win on what Reveal
  demonstrably does, not on fabricated gaps.
- Don't dilute the UK focus by chasing the US market first.
